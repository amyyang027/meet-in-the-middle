# Video outline (target 7–8 minutes, 4 speakers)

Split it however you like. A natural split is one section per person.

| Time | Section | What to show | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | Hook + problem | A Bay Area map with the 4 members' cities | "Every week we argue about where to practice." Explain lopsided distances, bridges, and traffic. |
| 1:00–2:30 | Formulation | Slide with sets, x_o, min-sum and min-max | Tie it to the median and center problems from class. Show why "fair" and "short total" disagree. |
| 2:30–3:45 | Non-class methods | Slide: CVaR picture (tail of a distribution) and OWA weights | CVaR = average of the worst 10% of days, as an LP (Rockafellar–Uryasev). OWA = weight the longest trips more. |
| 3:45–5:30 | Live demo | Screen recording of the app | Enter 4 cities, tick a host, click *Find our spot*. Walk through the comparison table. Turn on food (Korean). Plan an 8-week season. |
| 5:30–7:00 | Results | Figures 1–3 | Min total is unfair (+20% for the farthest person). The rotation IP beats same-spot by 11–23%. Be honest that CVaR rarely changes the answer. |
| 7:00–7:45 | Limits + next steps | One slide | Assumed traffic, OSM gaps, transit, prices. |

Tips: record the demo in **Demo mode** too, in case a free map server is slow on recording day.
Upload to Google Drive or Box and put the share link at the top of the PDF.
